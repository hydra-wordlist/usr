Inspect readable strings:

Scan the image with Binwalk:
binwalk firmware.bin
Attempt extraction when supported:
binwalk -e firmware.bin
Inspect extracted content:
find _firmware.bin.extracted -maxdepth 3 -type f | head -n 50
