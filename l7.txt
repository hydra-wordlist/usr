Identify the interface:
ip addr
Capture MQTT traffic :
sudo tcpdump -i lo -nn -s0 -w mqtt-lab.pcap port 1883
Generate several MQTT messages, then stop tcpdump with Ctrl+C.
Open the capture in Wireshark:
wireshark mqtt-lab.pcap
Apply display filter: `mqtt`.
Inspect CONNECT, SUBSCRIBE and PUBLISH packets.
Locate the topic and payload in a PUBLISH packet.
Repeat with CoAP traffic and use display filter: `coap` or `udp.port == 5683`.
